How to capture

  1. Stand a few steps back from the first outside corner. Move the phone slowly side to side for a few seconds so it can find the ground.
  2. Aim the crosshair where the wall meets the ground at the corner, hold steady, and tap Corner.
  3. Walk to the next corner (either direction) and repeat for every corner, including small jogs and bump-outs.
  4. Back at the start, tap Finish, aim at the first corner again and tap Close loop. That re-tap measures drift and corrects it.

Tips: keep within about 3-4 m (10-13 ft) of the corner you are aiming at. Walk at a normal pace with the camera pointing at the house or ground, not the sky. Daylight works best.
